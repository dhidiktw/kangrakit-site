Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

KangRakit is a personal content channel. The automation tool behind it posts pre-produced videos to the KangRakit account on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram — it does not operate a public-facing app or website that collects visitor data.

In plain terms: this tool has exactly one user (the channel owner), and it only ever posts to accounts the owner owns. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared by it.

What this covers

This policy applies to KangRakit's automated publishing tool and this website. It does not cover YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram themselves — any interaction with KangRakit's content on those platforms (comments, likes, views, follows) is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

Data collection

This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking of any kind. It's a static page with no backend.
